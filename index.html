Our story

A counter built on one idea

Sakura Sushi opened in 2016 in the heart of Skopje with a simple belief: sushi is a craft measured in years, not recipes.

Two decades of training in Tokyo, Osaka and Skopje sit behind every plate.

Rice is eighty percent of the nigiri. If the rice is wrong, nothing else matters.

Hiroshi Tanaka, Executive Head Chef
