Before you write

Do you take walk-ins?

The first four counter seats are always held for walk-ins. Everything else is reservation-only after 6pm.

Is there a vegetarian menu?

Yes — look for the green dot on the menu page. Our Vegetable Roll and Zen Set are both fully plant-based.

Do you deliver outside the city centre?

We deliver within a 6 km radius of the restaurant. Enter your address at checkout and we'll confirm.

Can I book the whole counter for a private event?

Yes, for up to eight guests. Send us a message with the date and we'll get back to you within a day.
